QR code for your card

Paste the public URL of your card page, then download or print. Test it with your phone camera before you print a batch.

Download PNG
Print tips Keep the code at least 2 cm (¾ in) wide on a business card and 5 cm (2 in) on a counter sign. Leave a white border around it. Dark code on a light background only. Put the URL in plain text under the code too, for anyone whose camera won't scan.
